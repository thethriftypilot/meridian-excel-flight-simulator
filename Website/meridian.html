Land, review and fly again

Choose a landing challenge, touch down on runway 15 and brake to a stop. Press Esc, then VIEW DEBRIEF for your game score, touchdown measurements and flight-path charts. REPLAY LAST FLIGHT lets you inspect the approach from five cameras.

Replay: P pauses, J/L seek five seconds, K changes speed and C changes camera. Replay is silent and keeps about 30 minutes in memory until the next flight or workbook close. Save your workbook to retain the debrief and personal bests. Assisted flights do not set records.

Get started

  1. Download and extract the complete ZIP to a writable local folder.
  2. Keep Assets beside ExcelFlightSimulator.xlsm.
  3. Open the workbook in 64-bit Windows desktop Excel and authorize macros for this trusted file.
  4. Click OPEN FLIGHT SIMULATOR, then START FLIGHT or WATCH DEMONSTRATION.

Requirements: Windows desktop Excel with VBA7; tested in 64-bit Excel 2021 on Windows 11. Mac and web Excel are not supported. No Python, network connection or external rendering engine is needed to fly. Do not enable all macros globally; VBA-project trust is only needed to rebuild.

Controls: Arrow keys fly · W/S power · F flaps · Space brakes · C camera · M map · P pause · E engine · Esc menu.

Tested, with clear limits

78 native Excel checks passed for v0.4, including scoring failures, recorder limits, replay controls, personal bests and report charts. The earlier v0.3 release completed a 20-minute continuous flight at 24.53 FPS on the development PC. Performance varies by computer; Balanced is sharper but slower.

Experimental entertainment software, not a training device or navigation source. Aircraft handling and airport buildings are simplified. Joystick controls, live METAR and broader manual/hardware testing remain outside this release.
