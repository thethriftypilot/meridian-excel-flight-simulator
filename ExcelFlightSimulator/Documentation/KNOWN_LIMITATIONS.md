# Known limitations — release 0.2

This is an integrated, playable engineering build, not completion of every feature in the original specification.

- The flight model uses lift/drag/thrust and gravity but simplified attitude dynamics. It does not model six-degree-of-freedom inertia, detailed propeller aerodynamics, spin recovery, ground effect, tire side forces, or certified Piper handling. Oil behavior and magnetic variation are approximations.
- The terrain is authentic and geographically aligned, but prepared at 20 m elevation and 5 m imagery resolution. It is a bare-earth heightfield, so trees and buildings are largely flat photographic texture. There are no separate airport buildings, taxiway routing, obstacle collisions, or custom land-cover geometry.
- The airport layout is visually aligned to aerial imagery and a dated FAA chart. Current surveyed runway endpoints and current airport data were not obtained. Runway polygons overlay the terrain without a general scenery depth buffer; hills may not fully occlude them from every off-airport viewpoint.
- The cockpit includes live airspeed, attitude, altitude, heading, RPM, vertical-speed, fuel, throttle, flaps, trim, approximate oil temperature/pressure, turn-rate/slip, and magnetic compass indications. It is a compact custom panel, not a photorealistic reproduction. The six-pack now includes analog vertical speed and turn/slip indications, plus a rotating magnetic heading card. Altitude uses a simplified single needle, and the turn indicator is an approximate banked bar rather than a modeled gyroscope.
- The chase aircraft is a simple banked 2D silhouette. Scenic view looks off-axis from the aircraft; it is not an independent free camera. Instrument view preserves round gauges and adds large numeric flight data. Perspective uses the customary heightfield small-angle pitch approximation.
- Weather is deterministic and offline. Day, sunset, night, haze, crosswind, and overcast/gust presets exist. Clouds are inexpensive projected shapes; there is no volumetric cloud penetration, rain particle system, real METAR, precipitation physics, or moving sun simulation.
- Audio is original synthesized mono PCM, with approximately 0.4 seconds of queued buffering. Engine harmonics, wind, rolling noise, and stall/overspeed tones are included. There are no separately recorded starter, switch, touchdown, or menu sounds. Subjective sound quality and all audio-device configurations have not been verified.
- Keyboard controls are implemented. There is no joystick integration or editable binding UI. Continuous controls poll physical key state; short taps get a 100 ms minimum pulse. Toggle keys use form events to avoid missed taps and keyboard-repeat dependence.
- Performance mode uses two-pixel spans, so terrain is visibly pixelated. Balanced is sharper but slower. No high-resolution experimental preset is exposed because benchmarks do not justify it.
- All terrain stays resident in memory. This deliberately avoids runtime tile churn for the compact package; it does not implement an expandable streaming world. The boundary pauses flight without an automatic route home.
- The application uses a large dedicated window, not a borderless full-screen shell. Excel may remain visible behind it. No global Excel UI settings are hidden or changed.
- 64-bit Windows Excel 2021 was tested. Mac, web Excel, 32-bit Office, multiple simultaneous simulator workbooks, and all DPI/audio-device combinations are not validated.
- Automated guidance demonstrated a complete approach and flare. Extended manual landing practice, crosswind landing validation, and long wall-clock memory/audio soak tests remain incomplete. All 30 camera/weather combinations have bounded native render coverage.

No FPS, screenshot, or automated test result in this package is a mockup. The visual frame captures were generated by the actual VBA runtime.
